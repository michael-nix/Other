### BADMATH

A holding place for some of my terrible MATLAB one-liners or worse implementations of standard things, such as:

 - `badcdf` - A terrible implementation of the Gaussian cumulative distribution function,
 - `badconv` - The worst way to do convolution,
 - `badcos` - There are worse ways to compute cosines,
 - `baderf` - The subject of, "A Slower, Less Accurate Error Function,"
 - `badgauss` - A terrible implementation of a Gaussian function,
 - `badhermite` - I mean, how else would one compute Hermite polynomials?
 - `badpdf` - Generate a probability 'density' function from some data,
 - `badrand` - For a given `badpdf`, generate random data with that distribution,
 - `badsin` There are better ways to compute sines.

I have a few more floating around in my head, but I don't quite have the time or the energy to write them down these days.

### old-school

I found a bunch of my old grad school stuff on a hard drive and figured I'd upload the more, "feature complete," documents.  I actually got to present at a couple of different conferences; Photonics North, Lasers and Electro-Optics Society, Optical Fiber Communications, some weird thing on campus.  Thought it would be neat to keep it here for the sake of posterity.

 - `LEOS 2006 DW-TDFA` - I think I did this before fourth year university; came up with a way to pump an optical fiber amplifer from... BOTH ENDS.  Yeah, high tech stuff.  It was actually pretty neat.

 - `OFC 2008 MMF-MFT` - I submitted this to OFC 2008, but I think this is the one I ended up presenting at Photonics North maybe, I don't remember.  We did a bunch of work trying to reduce modal noise in legacy multimode fiber links using crazy schemes; this one uses a tapered fiber ("manufactured" using a fusion splicer).

 - `OFC 2009 AT-IPS` - This one I got to fly down to San Diego to present at OFC which was pretty cool; a few hundred people in the audience.  Some guy in England wanted to work with me but at this time in my life I was desperate to leave academia so just kinda shrugged it off.  Did you know you can also use fiber tapers to send light down different paths?  Gasp, thrilling stuff.  Oh right, the university refused to reimburse me for the trip and I had to ask my dad to put it on his credit card.  God I hated grad school.

 - `Thesis` - This was my Master's thesis that I successfuly defended, but did not graduate as that would have required me to spend at least another year attempting to publish three more papers while paying tuition the whole time.  Considering I had already been working full time for six months in a completely different city at this time... lol, fuck that.
